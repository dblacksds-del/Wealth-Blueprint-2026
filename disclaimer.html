1. Honest Earnings Disclaimer

Building an online business carries financial and operational risk. Please understand our core policy:

  • Zero Income Promises: We do not make any guarantees or promises regarding how much money you will earn. Most beginners earn little or nothing when they first start. Success depends on your effort, persistence, communication ability, and external market conditions.
  • Illustrative Examples Only: Any dollar figures, hourly rates, or profit margins shown in our guides and interactive calculators are examples only with editable assumptions. They are provided strictly to illustrate unit economic formulas, not to predict your future income.
  • Not Licensed Professional Advice: Content published on this website is for educational and informational purposes only. It should not be construed as legal, tax, accounting, or professional financial advice. Always consult a qualified professional in your jurisdiction before making significant financial commitments.

2. FTC Affiliate Commission Disclosure

In compliance with the United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines (16 CFR Part 255), please note:

  • Some of the links on WealthBlueprint are affiliate links. This means that if you click an outbound link and make a purchase or subscribe to a software service (such as Shopify, Printify, or web hosting providers), we may earn a small referral commission.
  • Zero Extra Cost to You: Clicking an affiliate link will never increase the price you pay. In many cases, it may offer you a discounted trial or special promotional rate.
  • Editorial Independence: We only recommend software and services that we believe provide genuine utility to beginners. We do not accept paid placements for products we consider poor value.
