The Mathematical Unit Economics Formula

Every venture ranked by our interactive tool is evaluated using our standardized 2026 Net Venture Yield (NVY) Equation:

Net Monthly Profit (\$) = [Monthly Traffic × Conversion Rate (%) × AOV (\$)] − [COGS + CAC + Platform Fees]

Where each variable represents real-world platform mechanics:

  • Conversion Rate (%): Benchmarked between 1.8% and 5.0% depending on whether discovery occurs through algorithmic video (TikTok Shop) or search intent (Google AI Overviews).
  • Average Order Value (AOV): Ranges from $27.00 for micro-digital templates to $350.00 for B2B monthly retainers.
  • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): Maintained at $0.00 for organic content creation or budgeted at $6.50 to $14.20 on performance ad testing.
  • Platform Fees: Explicitly accounts for Stripe processing (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction), TikTok Shop merchant commissions (6.0% to 8.0%), and Shopify base subscriptions.

Step-by-Step Decision Workflow

To select the highest-probability business model from your quiz output, execute this sequential 4-step workflow:

  1. Step 1: Determine Liquidity Constraints: If your liquid bank balance is under $50 USD, immediately eliminate physical inventory models. Prioritize Zero-Capital pathways (Affiliate Marketing or B2B Freelancing) to generate initial cash reserves.
  2. Step 2: Calculate Weekly Hourly Labor Arbitrage: Operators with under 10 hours weekly should deploy high-margin asynchronous assets (Digital Products or AI Automation Chatbots) rather than high-maintenance e-commerce storefronts.
  3. Step 3: Stress-Test Platform Concentration: Avoid relying entirely on a single platform algorithm. Pair social commerce discovery (e.g., TikTok) with owned assets (an email list on Beehiiv or an authority website hosted on independent infrastructure).
  4. Step 4: Execute the 30-Day Testing Sprint: Dedicate the first 30 days exclusively to audience feedback and unit economics verification before investing additional capital into software subscriptions.

What Are the 10 Proven Ways to Make Money Online in 2026?

1. AI Dropshipping ($1–$80/mo Startup; $35–$59 US AOV)

Dropshipping in 2026 has transitioned completely away from unoptimized general storefronts with 20-day shipping windows. Successful modern operators use AI visual generators (such as Midjourney v6 and Stable Diffusion) to create photorealistic lifestyle product imagery, integrating directly with domestic fulfillment centers offering 3-to-5-day delivery SLAs. Average conversion rates across TikTok Shop storefronts range from 3.2% to 5.0%, generating average order values between $35 and $59 USD (TikTok Shop Seller Center).
