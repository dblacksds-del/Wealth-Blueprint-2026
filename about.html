Why This Website Was Built

If you have ever searched for ways to make money online, you have probably encountered two frustrating extremes:

  • Over-hyped "get-rich-quick" gurus who rent supercars, flash fake dashboard screenshots, and charge $997 for vague video courses.
  • Academic corporate jargon filled with complex business school terms that fail to explain how a real person sets up an online store or gets their first client.

WealthBlueprint was created by Dhanushka Lakshan Senanayake to bridge that gap. Our mission is to explain modern digital business models in simple, 13-year-old reading level sentences that anyone can follow. We give you exact startup costs, transparent risk disclosures, and step-by-step instructions with zero false promises.

How Our Guides Are Researched & Created

We believe in complete transparency about our editorial process. Every guide on this website follows a clear standard:

  1. Original Outlines & Real Research: Topics are selected based on real beginner challenges. We audit software tools (like Make.com, Printify, Shopify, and Canva) and document their actual current pricing and policies.
  2. AI Assistance for Drafting & Coding: We use artificial intelligence models as assistive tools to help write clean code, generate interactive calculators, and produce initial text drafts.
  3. Strict Human Review & Fact Checking: Every page is reviewed by Dhanushka Lakshan Senanayake to verify mathematical accuracy, remove hype, ensure simple readability, and enforce our project rule: zero fabricated statistics or fake claims.

How This Website Makes Money

Running a website involves web hosting, domain registration, and ongoing research expenses. We monetize WealthBlueprint through three transparent channels:

1. Display Advertising (Google AdSense):

We display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense across our free guides. When visitors view or click relevant ads, we receive a small payment from the advertiser.

2. Occasional Affiliate Partnerships:

Some links to software tools (such as Printify, Shopify, or web hosting platforms) are affiliate links. If you choose to subscribe to a service through our link, we may earn a small referral commission at zero extra cost to you. We only recommend tools that provide genuine utility.

3. Optional Offline Guide Book ($12 USD):

For readers who want an ad-free downloadable PDF handbook, printable desk worksheets, and pre-formatted spreadsheet templates, we offer an optional Starter Guide Book backed by a 14-day refund guarantee.
