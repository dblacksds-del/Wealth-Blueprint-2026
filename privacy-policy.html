Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy explains what basic information is collected when you visit WealthBlueprint, how cookies work, and what choices you have regarding your personal data.

DS
Written by Dhanushka Lakshan Senanayake, with AI assistance. Reviewed October 2026.
Practical digital venture education with realistic expectations and zero false promises.

1. What Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver and improve our educational guides:

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: If you send us a message through our contact form, subscribe to our email newsletter, or purchase our optional Guide Book, you provide basic details like your name and email address.
  • Standard Server Logs: When you access any page on the web, hosting servers record basic technical data like your IP address, browser type, device operating system, referring URL, and time of visit. This data is used solely to maintain site uptime and prevent malicious attacks.
  • Local Browser Storage: We use your browser's localStorage to remember your theme preference (Dark mode vs. Light mode). This data stays entirely on your computer and is never sent to our servers.

2. Google AdSense & Cookie Disclosures

WealthBlueprint uses Google AdSense to display advertisements across our pages. As part of this service:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • How to Opt Out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's official Google Ad Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Website Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics tools (such as Google Analytics with IP anonymization enabled) to understand which guides are most helpful to our readers. This software records aggregated statistics like page views, reading duration, and general geographical region. It does not identify individual visitors personally.

4. Email Newsletter Privacy

If you subscribe to our weekly venture notes, your email address is stored securely with our email delivery provider (such as MailerLite or Beehiiv). We will never sell, rent, or trade your email address with third parties. Every email we send includes a clear, working 1-click unsubscribe link at the bottom so you can opt out instantly at any time.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Whether you are located in the United States (including California), the United Kingdom, or the European Union, you have specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you (such as your email subscriber record).
  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request that we delete your email or contact submission records from our databases.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or barter your personal information to data brokers or third parties for monetary or other considerations.
  • Non-Discrimination: Exercising your privacy rights will never affect your ability to read our free guides or use our website.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us through our Contact Page and we will process your request within 30 days.
