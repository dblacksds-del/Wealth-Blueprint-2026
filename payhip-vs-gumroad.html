The Mathematical Crossover Points: When to Upgrade

Upgrading to a paid monthly plan before your store produces consistent revenue wastes capital. You can calculate the exact break-even point where paying a fixed monthly fee saves you more money than paying per-transaction cuts:

Payhip Free to Payhip Plus Crossover Math: $966.67 / Month

Payhip Plus costs $29.00 USD per month. It reduces the transaction fee from 5% to 2%, giving you a 3% fee reduction (0.03).

Break-Even Point = $29.00 ÷ 0.03 = $966.67 USD Gross Sales

If your shop generates less than $967 in monthly sales, staying on Payhip Free is cheaper. Once your sales exceed $967/month, Payhip Plus saves you money.

Payhip Plus to Payhip Pro Crossover Math: $3,500.00 / Month

Payhip Pro costs $99.00 USD per month ($70 more than Plus). It drops the fee from 2% to 0%, saving 2% (0.02).

Break-Even Point = $70.00 ÷ 0.02 = $3,500.00 USD Gross Sales

Only consider Payhip Pro once your digital storefront is earning over $3,500 in consistent monthly revenue.

Merchant of Record (MoR) vs. Direct Gateway: The Tax Difference

The biggest operational difference between Gumroad and Payhip is who acts as the legal merchant when an international customer buys your digital download:

How Gumroad Operates as a Full Merchant of Record:

When a buyer in Germany, the UK, or California purchases from Gumroad, Gumroad legally sells the item and issues the invoice under Gumroad's business entity. Gumroad calculates the exact local sales tax or VAT, collects it from the customer, files tax returns, and remits the money directly to international tax authorities. For creators who want zero tax paperwork headaches, this is a major advantage.

How Payhip Handles Digital VAT and Direct Gateways:

On Payhip, customer payments deposit directly into your personal or business Stripe / PayPal account. Payhip automatically adds and collects European Union (EU) and United Kingdom (UK) digital VAT at checkout, and generates detailed periodic tax reports. However, because you are the merchant of record on Stripe, you are responsible for monitoring local state tax thresholds if you exceed sales nexus limits.

How to Deliver Google Sheets & Canva Templates Safely

Regardless of whether you choose Payhip or Gumroad, never upload raw editable template links directly into customer confirmation screens. Follow this safe delivery architecture:

  1. The Google Sheet Copy Link Trick: If selling a Google Sheet tracker, never give buyers an "Anyone with link can edit" link. If you do, the first buyer who types data will overwrite your master file for all future buyers! Instead, take your shared view link and replace /edit at the very end of the URL with /copy. When the buyer clicks it, Google automatically opens a page asking: "Would you like to make a copy of [Template Name]?" and saves a private copy directly into their personal Google Drive.
  2. The 1-Page PDF Delivery Document: In free Canva, create a clean 1-page PDF titled "Access Your [Product Name] Purchase". Include a prominent clickable button linking to your /copy URL, 3 bullet points explaining how to use it, and your support email (contact@wealthblueprinthub.com).
  3. Upload the PDF as the Primary Product File: In your Payhip or Gumroad product dashboard, upload that 1-page PDF. When a customer purchases, the platform delivers the PDF instantly, giving the customer a permanent file on their computer and preventing link tampering.
  4. Test in an Incognito Browser Window: Always test your PDF and copy links in an incognito/private browser window where you are not logged into your Google account. Confirm the template opens cleanly without asking for permission.

Day 1 Decision Framework: Which Platform Should You Choose?

Use this simple decision rule based on your setup:

Pick Payhip Free (5%) If:

  • You already have a verified Stripe or PayPal Business account in the US, UK, or Canada.
  • You want to maximize your net take-home cash and keep an extra ~$1.00 per $10 transaction.
  • You prefer immediate direct deposits into your bank rather than waiting for weekly payout cycles.
  • You plan to offer a multi-module online video course with student login tracking.

Pick Gumroad Direct (10% + $0.50) If:

  • You live in a country where Stripe is not supported, or you do not want to manage individual Stripe webhooks.
  • You want one single platform to act as the legal Merchant of Record and handle 100% of global sales tax filings.
  • You want your product indexed in Gumroad's searchable Discover catalog for potential organic marketplace discovery.
